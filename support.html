CartSplit Support

Help with the CartSplit grocery price app.

Getting started

Add items to your list, pick the stores you shop at (Settings, Where you shop), then tap Find Best Prices & Split. CartSplit shows the cheapest place for each item and only adds another store when it saves enough to be worth the trip.

Prices look wrong for a store

Open Settings, tap the store and pick the branch you shop at on its website. Online prices can differ from shelf prices, especially through Instacart.

Wrong product matched

Tap the item and choose "Must be this brand", or change how it's described. Generic items like milk match the cheapest of any brand.

Photo scanner

Barcode scanning is free and needs nothing. To identify a product from a photo, add a free Google Gemini key in Settings: tap Get a free key from Google, sign in, tap Create API key and paste it in.
