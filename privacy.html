Privacy Policy

CartSplit, by Joseph Huntley / capableprocess.ai. Effective November 10, 2026.

The short version

CartSplit has no servers of its own. Your grocery list, your stores and your settings are saved only on your iPhone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your information.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your grocery list, the stores you pick, your ZIP code or area, prices you type in, and your recent items.
  • If you sign in with Apple: the name and email Apple gives the app, used only to show who is signed in. Signing in is optional, and signing out removes them.
  • Your Google Gemini key, if you add one, kept in the iPhone Keychain.

Your location

If you tap Use My Location, the app reads your location once to find the nearest stores using Apple Maps. Your location is not saved off your phone and is not sent to us. You can enter a ZIP code instead.

Looking up prices

To find prices, the app searches the websites of the stores you choose (for example Wegmans, Aldi, Tops, Amazon, and Instacart for some stores) for the items on your list, the same way a web browser would. Those websites see the item searches and your device's internet address, and may set their own cookies, as when you visit them yourself. Their own privacy policies apply.

Barcodes and photos

  • When you scan a barcode, the number is looked up in the free Open Food Facts and UPCitemdb product databases.
  • When you photograph a product or check online prices, the photo or the product name is sent to Google's Gemini service using your own Gemini key. Google's terms and privacy policy apply, and on Google's free plan Google may use what is sent to improve its products. Only photograph groceries.

Children

CartSplit is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your information

Use Clear list and Sign out in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it saved.

Changes and contact
